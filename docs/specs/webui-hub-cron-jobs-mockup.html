## Schedules
Schedules are prompts sent to one or more agents on a timer.
They used to be called cron jobs. "Schedule", "cron job" and "cron" all mean the same thing.
The user sees them in Settings, Schedules (web) and in /schedules (terminal).
The tags keep their old names: hub_cron_add, hub_cron_list and the other hub_cron_* tags.
When you talk to the user, say schedule, not cron job.

I1 · 40 ideas for the Schedules tab. Ideas 1 to 12 are in this version. Ideas 13 to 40 are not, each with the reason.

40 ideas

In v13

  1. The status word replaces the switch: green Active, gray Paused.
  2. The status word is the button. Under the pointer it gets an underline (1b).
  3. Card text uses the full width; only the title line leaves room for the status.
  4. How often, in plain words: Every hour, Every 5 minutes.
  5. The next fire time on the card.
  6. Errors are red text only, no strip behind them.
  7. Waiting (device off the network) is gray text.
  8. The detail shows the next three fire times.
  9. The detail shows the last run and how it went (planned).
  10. The same status button in the card, the detail header and the form.
  11. A shorter summary line: 3 schedules · 1 paused · 1 waiting.
  12. A paused card is dimmed.

Not in v13, and why

  1. A Sunday to Saturday strip: schedules repeat on an interval and have no clock times yet. It fits once clock-time schedules exist.
  2. Clock-time schedules (9am on weekdays): a new backend feature, its own issue.
  3. Run history as 12 small blocks (green ok, red failed): needs a run log first.
  4. Run now in the detail: needs a hub call that fires a schedule once.
  5. A fix button in the error strip (Grant lapis access): needs the grant flow to exist on the web.
  6. Search above the list: worth it at 10 or more schedules.
  7. Filter Active, Paused, Errors: same, at 10 or more.
  8. Sort by next fire: the default order may already be this; decide in code.
  9. Group cards by network: only helps with several networks.
  10. An error count on the Schedules item in the Settings sidebar.
  11. The agent's gem color beside its name, matching the chat sidebar.
  12. Hover on a time shows the other form (in 30m and 10:30).
  13. Pause all and resume all.
  14. Duplicate a schedule.
  15. Copy one schedule as JSON, in the import format.
  16. Export all schedules to a file.
  17. Undo after Delete instead of a confirm.
  18. Starter schedules in the empty state (daily standup, tunnel check), added in one press.
  19. Templates in Add schedule.
  20. Keyboard: up and down move through cards, space flips the status.
  21. Phone: list and detail as two screens with Back, instead of stacked.
  22. Edit Every in place by pressing it.
  23. The prompt expands on the card under the pointer.
  24. Show who scheduled it on the card (it is in the detail now).
  25. A time zone label next to clock times.
  26. Last edited time in the detail.
  27. A countdown ring to the next fire: decoration, skipped.
  28. Drag to reorder: order means nothing for schedules, skipped.

Schedules

A schedule sends one prompt to one or more agents on a timer.

How to get started

  1. 1Open Settings, then Schedules, and press Add schedule.
  2. 2Type the prompt, set Every to 30s, 5m, 1h or 2h30m, and select the agents under Runs in.
  3. 3Keep Active on and press Save schedule. For many at once, press Import file.

 Generate with agent
 An agent drafts the schedule from your words. Nothing is saved until you review it.

   Describe it
     every 2 hours, ask lapis for a one-line status

   Ask agent     koordinator
   Repeats on an interval only: 30s, 5m, 1h or 2h30m. Clock times like 9am aren't supported.

   Draft from koordinator
     Prompt        Give me a one-line status.
     Runs in       lapis
     Every         2h
     Enabled       no, starts off

 enter draft   r review in form   d discard   esc cancel
